# Code of conduct

AttackGraph aims to be a respectful, technically rigorous project that anyone can take part
in. This code of conduct applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions,
and any other channel used for project communication.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and considerate, including when you disagree.
- Critique ideas and code, not people. Assume good faith.
- Be precise and honest about technical claims, evidence and uncertainty.
- Welcome newcomers and help them understand the project's engineering expectations.
- Respect confidentiality: do not share other people's private information, and follow
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for anything security-sensitive.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory language or conduct of any kind.
- Personal attacks, insults, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Using project spaces to promote attacks on systems you are not authorized to assess, or
  sharing exploit material or data from real environments.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainer ([@WiseHax](https://github.com/WiseHax)) is responsible for enforcing this
code of conduct and may remove, edit or reject contributions and comments, or restrict
participation, for behaviour that violates it.

To report a concern, contact the maintainer privately using a contact method listed on their
GitHub profile. If none is available, open a minimal issue asking for a private contact,
without including details of the incident. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical.
